Wholesale Expansion

How Candēla expanded from direct-to-consumer sales into retail and wholesale.

Project Challenge

Candēla had grown through e-commerce and direct sales, but expanding into retail required a different approach to pricing, packaging, and product positioning.

Solution

I prepared Candēla for B2B wholesale and debuted the brand at the Canadian Gift Association Trade Show, introducing new packaging, eight product collections, and retail-focused pricing.

CanGift Trade Show / Product Image

Results & Key Outcomes

Candēla grew to 16,000+ units sold across e-commerce, wholesale, and retail channels.

  • 23 retail partnerships
  • Retail presence across 7 Canadian provinces and territories
  • 60%+ increase in cumulative units sold after the August 2026 wholesale expansion

Retail partners include Linen Chest, Hanji Gifts, Eugène Allard, Boutique Première, Bébé Loup, Sprout Gift Co., The Hygge, Sunday State, and Murdoch's Gem Shop.
